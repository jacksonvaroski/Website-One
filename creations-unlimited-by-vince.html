What we build

Kitchens, baths, closets, and furniture — cut for your space, not a showroom.

Every job starts with a measurement, not a catalog page. Below is the full scope of what comes out of the shop.

01

Custom cabinet design & installation

Kitchen and bath cabinetry designed around your layout and built in the shop, then installed and leveled on-site.

02

Cabinet refacing & refinishing

New doors, veneers, and finishes over your existing boxes — a full kitchen look without a full demolition.

03

Cabinet painting

Sprayed, factory-grade finishes in the color and sheen you want, done in a controlled shop environment.

04

Cabinet repair

Sagging doors, water damage, broken drawer boxes — repaired and matched to the surrounding cabinetry.

05

Custom closet design & installation

Built-in closet systems sized to your actual wardrobe and space, not a modular kit.

06

Closet woodworking

Solid-wood shelving, drawer inserts, and finish carpentry for reach-ins, walk-ins, and pantries.

Since 1993
